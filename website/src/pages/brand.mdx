**Ritmo** is rhythm as discipline, not mood: a beat that never drifts. Every
tick lands on the grid, every system plays its part in a checked order, and
changes land only on explicit beats.

- **The icon** is the world as cells: three blocks an entity is built from,
  and one smaller cell, the one that changes this tick. It reads as ECS and as
  pixel art, and survives one colour at 16px.
- **The wordmark** is drawn from fixed strokes (no font), so it is identical
  everywhere. The dot of the `i` is the icon's small cell. The ruler
  underneath says *measured*.

## Type

A monospace face for headings and code (JetBrains Mono or IBM Plex Mono), and
a sans for body text (Inter or IBM Plex Sans). This site uses JetBrains Mono
and Inter.

## Rules

- **Accent marks the changing cell.** Tick is for the icon's small square and
  the wordmark's dot. The round avatar is the only solid accent background.
- **Clear space.** Keep one icon cell (a big square) of empty space around the
  icon and the lockups.
- **Minimum sizes.** The icon at 16px. The wordmark with the ruler at 120px
  wide; below that, use the compact wordmark.
- **Precision, not music.** No notes, waves, headphones, or soft gradients.
  Grids, ticks, and blocks only.
- **Words.** Say step, tick, order, exact, checked, declared, deterministic,
  replay. Avoid flow, groove, vibe, harmony, and musical puns.
