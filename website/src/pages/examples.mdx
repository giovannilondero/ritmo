## Top-down

A small world to walk around: five parts to find, walls to slide along. The
rules (movement, collision, what is in reach, picking up) are the simulation
in [`integration/top-down`](https://github.com/typeonce-dev/ritmo/tree/main/integration/top-down),
the same schedules its headless tests run. The page adds the loop, the
keyboard and gamepad, and a Pixi renderer.

<Game id="top-down" />

Read how the page puts it together in
[Loop, input, rendering](/docs/browser/), and how its tests drive it without a
browser in [Headless debugging](/docs/devtools/).

## Games built with Ritmo

**[Spirit Call](https://spirit-call.typeonce.dev)**: a top-down action game
for the phone, with teams of elemental spirits, rendered in 3D with Three.js
and shipped to Android and iOS.
[Play it](https://spirit-call.typeonce.dev) or read
[its source](https://github.com/SandroMaglione/spirit-call).

**[Inkline Town](https://github.com/SandroMaglione/inkline-town)**: a town
simulation in isometric line art, rendered with Three.js.
